# NASA Data & Resources for the "Flame in Freefall" challenge

- PSI Data Repository: Combustion Science — https://psi.nasa.gov/physci/repo/search?q=combustion — Physical Sciences Informatics (PSI) source of the data upon which the NASA Space App is to be based.
- NASA Combustion Science — https://www.nasa.gov/glenn/glenn-expertise-space-exploration/physical-sciences-program/combustion-science/ — Basic information about most of the combustion investigations for which data is found in the PSI Data Repository.
- NASA ScienceCasts: The Power of Studying Combustion on the ISS — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W1oXykoz9zg — Introduction to NASA's combustion research on the International Space Station through a 3.6-minute video clip from 2018.
- A Researcher's Guide to International Space Station Combustion Science — https://www.nasa.gov/missions/station/a-researchers-guide-to-combustion-science/ — A 2015 review of NASA's combustion research conducted on, or planned for, the ISS. Basic information about most of the combustion investigations described in this 50+ page document is found within the PSI Data Repository.
- Apollo 1 — https://sma.nasa.gov/sma-disciplines/accllp/apollo-1 — Crew, mission, fire that erupted during a pre-launch test killing the crew, investigation, and lessons learned.
- Apollo 13 — https://sma.nasa.gov/sma-disciplines/accllp/apollo-13 — Crew, mission, oxygen tank explosion during the flight to the Moon, investigation, and lessons learned.
- 25 Years Ago: Fire Aboard Space Station Mir — https://www.nasa.gov/history/25-years-ago-fire-aboard-space-station-mir/ — Fire caused by an oxygen generator failure aboard Mir in 1997.
- Fireman's Air Tanks — https://spinoff.nasa.gov/node/9582 — 1970s adaptation of spacesuit technology to improve firefighters' air tanks and breathing gear.
- Fire Detector — https://spinoff.nasa.gov/node/9407 — 1970s shuttle smoke detector that discriminates between smoke and dust (fewer nuisance alarms; used e.g. in mining).
- New Technology for the Fire Attire — https://spinoff.nasa.gov/node/9918 — 1980s application of NASA spacesuit technology to improve firefighter clothing.

Note (from the page): NASA does not endorse any non-U.S. Government entity and is not responsible for information contained on non-U.S. Government websites.
