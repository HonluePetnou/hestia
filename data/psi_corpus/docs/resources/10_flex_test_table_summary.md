# FLEX droplet tests: what the test table shows

Counts and medians computed from the 276 FLEX droplet tests listed in Table IX of NASA/TP-2015-216046 (ISS, Combustion Integrated Rack, Multi-user Droplet Combustion Apparatus, March 2009 to December 2011). Fuels: methanol and n-heptane.

## Outcomes by fuel
- methanol: 157 tests; flame extinction 126 (80%), droplet disruption 23 (15%), complete burn 8 (5%).
- n-heptane: 119 tests; flame extinction 60 (50%), droplet disruption 39 (33%), complete burn 20 (17%).

Which fuel extinguishes more often: in FLEX, 80% of methanol tests ended in flame extinction versus 50% of n-heptane tests. n-heptane droplets were more often disrupted (the droplet broke apart or the test ended early) than methanol droplets.

## Extinction diameter (how small the droplet was when the flame went out)
- methanol: over 103 extinction tests, the flame went out when the droplet had shrunk to a median 0.57 of its initial diameter (middle half 0.47 to 0.82).
- n-heptane: over 56 extinction tests, the flame went out when the droplet had shrunk to a median 0.84 of its initial diameter (middle half 0.76 to 0.96).

n-heptane flames therefore went out much earlier in the burn (at a larger droplet) than methanol flames, which typically burned until the droplet was a bit over half its size.

## Oxygen level and outcome
- methanol: tests span 12% to 34% oxygen; the lowest oxygen at which a test did not end in flame extinction was 18%.
  - 15% O2 or less: 37 tests, 100% ended in extinction.
  - 16-18% O2: 36 tests, 97% ended in extinction.
  - 19-21% O2: 49 tests, 84% ended in extinction.
  - above 21% O2: 35 tests, 37% ended in extinction.
- n-heptane: tests span 13% to 34% oxygen; the lowest oxygen at which a test did not end in flame extinction was 14%.
  - 15% O2 or less: 12 tests, 67% ended in extinction.
  - 16-18% O2: 31 tests, 74% ended in extinction.
  - 19-21% O2: 43 tests, 51% ended in extinction.
  - above 21% O2: 33 tests, 21% ended in extinction.

Lower oxygen made extinction more likely for both fuels.

## Burning rate K
- methanol: median burning-rate constant K = 0.46 mm²/s over 151 tests.
- n-heptane: median burning-rate constant K = 0.54 mm²/s over 109 tests.
- Across both fuels, median K was 0.37 mm²/s at 16% oxygen or less versus 0.55 mm²/s at 21% or more: droplets burn faster in richer oxygen.

## Diluents and pressure
- Carbon dioxide (CO2) was added in 123 tests (5% to 70% CO2); 72% of them ended in extinction, versus 57% of the nitrogen-only tests.
- Helium was added in 51 tests (5% to 45% He); median K 0.60 mm²/s versus 0.50 mm²/s in nitrogen-only atmospheres.
- Pressure ranged from 70 to 309 kPa (0.69 to 3.05 atm).

All figures are descriptive statistics of the published test table, not model predictions.
